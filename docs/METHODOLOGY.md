# Methodology

This repository is a curated directory, not an absolute ranking. It separates the type of an entity from its research or product focus so that, for example, a university laboratory and a company can both be discovered under `robotics` without being treated as equivalent organizations.

## Inclusion

An entry must satisfy at least one of these conditions:

- sustained scientific influence;
- development of notable models, methods, infrastructure, datasets, or benchmarks;
- strong open-source contribution;
- international or substantial regional importance;
- high educational value;
- authoritative specialist coverage of a field underrepresented by general AI lists.

Popularity alone is not enough. Subscriber counts, funding, valuations, and social reach can be signals, but do not replace scientific or practical relevance.

## Tiers

- **A — Global leader:** field-defining organization or person with sustained global impact.
- **B — Major:** internationally recognized or regionally leading contributor.
- **C — Specialist:** authoritative specialist source or organization.
- **D — Emerging:** promising and relevant, but too new for sustained-impact claims.
- **Reference:** conferences, journals, databases, and similar resources that should not be ranked as companies or people.

Tiers are broad discovery aids. They are not ordinal league tables.

## Verification

Each accepted row has an official website or primary profile and a `verification_url`. Social accounts are included only when they can be connected to an official site, institutional profile, or clearly established identity. A successful HTTP response is useful but is not proof of ownership.

`verified_at` records the last editorial verification date. Projects can be marked `historical` instead of being silently removed.

## Geographic balance

Coverage is reviewed across North America, Latin America, Europe, Africa, the Middle East, South Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia, and Oceania. Global prominence and geographic diversity are considered together; regional entries are not required to mimic the US commercial ecosystem.

## Maintenance

Automated checks catch malformed URLs, duplicate IDs, invalid enums, and missing required fields. Editorial review remains necessary for ownership, significance, descriptions, and tier assignments.
